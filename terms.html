2. Architecture & One-Way Mirroring Guarantee

OneSync is designed strictly as a one-way cloud-to-local synchronization and backup client. The application uses only read-level OAuth permissions (specifically Files.Read and User.Read). Under no operational state does OneSync upload local modifications back to your Microsoft OneDrive cloud, nor does it issue deletion, rename, or alteration requests against remote OneDrive storage.

While OneSync implements atomic downloads via temporary files, cryptographic verification (SHA-256 and QuickXorHash), and quarantines displaced local files to a dedicated restored/ folder, users remain responsible for maintaining adequate backups of their hardware and storage devices.
